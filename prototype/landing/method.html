How Bazodiac Relationships handles perspectives and evidence

At Bazodiac Relationships, we reject the notion of reducing the complexity of human connection to an objective compatibility percentage or a deterministic grade. Instead, our system serves as a structured framework to support mutual reflection.

1. Separation of Calculation and Interpretation Layers

Calculations and interpretations are handled as completely independent architectural layers. Raw celestial and metaphysical coordinates are resolved strictly within their native mathematical contexts before any relationship rules are evaluated.

2. Distinction of Source Systems

Chinese metaphysics and Western astrological calculations remain strictly distinct. We respect the historical, mathematical, and philosophical integrity of both traditions, mapping their insights as discrete, non-blended angles of perspective rather than mixing them into an artificial hybrid.

3. Independent Signal Verification

We employ strict validation rules. Interrelated signals originating from the same system family (for example, overlapping aspects from the same planetary cluster) are recognized as dependent and are not counted as independent confirmations. This prevents the inflating of minor features into false patterns.

4. Traceability of Claims

Every relational dynamic displayed in a report must possess absolute traceability back to its originating astronomical coordinates, astrological source features, and reviewed mapping rules. Users can inspect precisely which structural factors led to a specific interpretation.

5. Handling of Conflict and Contradiction

Contradictions and tensions within the data remain fully visible. Human relationships contain natural paradoxes—for example, a high affinity for shared action alongside contrasting communication styles. We do not smooth over these contradictions; we present them as equal, active dynamics.

6. Abstention Over Manufacturing Certainty

When evidence is insufficient, contradictory, or has not been rigorously reviewed by our team, the system explicitly abstains from making a claim. We believe that presenting an honest question mark is infinitely more valuable than fabricating speculative certainty.

7. Why We Do Not Offer a Compatibility Score

An objective compatibility percentage is a deterministic verdict that limits agency. Relationships are co-created interactions, not static equations. By focusing on "What may emerge between us, under which conditions, and from which perspectives?", we empower individuals to understand their shared dynamic rather than judging its longevity.
