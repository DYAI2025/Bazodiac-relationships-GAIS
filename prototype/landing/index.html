Individual Tendency

Earlier verbal processing

This sample perspective represents a tendency to clarify experience through relatively early dialogue. It is not a fixed trait and must be compared with lived experience.

Context-dependent interaction

Different processing tempo

One person may seek early dialogue while the other first needs time and internal distance. Pressure for immediate clarification can intensify withdrawal; unlimited withdrawal can intensify the experience of contact loss.

Possible intensifying conditions:

  • time pressure;
  • unclear pause expectations;
  • repeated demands for immediate resolution;
  • withdrawal without a defined return point.

Possible moderating conditions:

  • an agreed pause;
  • a specific time to reconnect;
  • naming the need for reflection without ending contact;
  • allowing both dialogue and processing time.
Counter-hypothesis

The observed pattern may also be shaped by current stress, learned communication habits, context, or unequal expectations rather than the interpreted source features.
